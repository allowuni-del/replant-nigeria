About us

Replant Nigeria Limited collects, sorts and bales recyclable materials and supplies them to manufacturers and recycling mills in Nigeria. We work with local suppliers and collectors and pay promptly for quality material.

What we buy

  • Cartons and corrugated boxes
  • White paper and mixed waste paper
  • PET plastic bottles
  • Woven sacks (polypropylene bags)
